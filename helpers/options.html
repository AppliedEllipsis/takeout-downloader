⚙️ Takeout Helper Settings

When enabled, opening the popup is optional — every new capture goes straight to your clipboard. Recommended if you keep the popup closed most of the time.
After a capture, the toolbar icon gets a small text overlay showing the last 6 characters of the filename. Helpful when juggling multiple Takeout jobs in different browser tabs.

What this extension does

Watches network requests to takeout.google.com, takeout-download.usercontent.google.com, and storage.cloud.google.com. When you click any download button on the Takeout page, it captures the URL and the full Cookie header, plus any of User-Agent, Accept, Accept-Language, and Referer that Chrome sent.
Nothing is sent to any server. You copy the JSON (or cURL) from the popup and paste it into the Takeout Downloader TUI.
Cookie-redirect gotcha: the request that the extension needs to capture is the one to takeout-download.usercontent.google.com — the FINAL host after Chrome follows the redirect chain. If the popup shows a "Pre-redirect capture" warning, your downloads will return an HTML login page instead of a ZIP. Pause the download in Chrome's Downloads tab, open DevTools → Network, and copy the takeout-download.usercontent.google.com request instead.
