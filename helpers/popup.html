1. Open takeout.google.com
2. Click any Download button (just one — we capture the cookie)
3. Click the extension icon → Copy ALL exports to copy a multi-payload with every archive on your account
4. Paste into the Takeout Downloader CLI (Ctrl+Shift+V)

Copy ALL fetches the full export list from Takeout's server using your captured cookie. The CLI shows a menu to pick which archive to download (sorted smallest first).
